Our promise to you

The Calm Visit Promise. For every patient, every visit.

However long it's been, here's what you can count on every time you're in our chair.

  1. We explain first

    Before anything happens, we tell you what we see, what we'd suggest and why, in plain language.

  2. You set the pace

    Need a break? Raise your hand and we stop. No rushing, ever.

  3. No judgment

    Six months or six years since your last visit, we're just glad you're here. No lectures.

  4. No surprises

    You'll know the plan before we start. Nothing happens you didn't agree to.

Common questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most PPO dental plans. Call (801) 467-6555 with your plan name and we'll confirm whether we're in network before your visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

Join our in-office membership plan. Your exams, cleanings and X-rays are covered, with savings on other treatment. Ask our front desk for details.

What should I bring?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take.

It's been years since my last visit. Is that okay?

Completely. No lectures and no judgment. We'll start where you are.
