Nervous about the dentist?

The Calm Visit Promise. Made for anxious patients.

Dr. Day did her residency at the Salt Lake City VA hospital, caring for veterans living with PTSD and dental anxiety. That's where our whole approach comes from.

  1. We explain first

    Before anything happens, we tell you what we see, what we'd suggest and why, in plain language.

  2. You set the pace

    Need a break? Raise your hand and we stop. No rushing, ever.

  3. No judgment

    Six months or six years since your last visit, we're just glad you're here. No lectures.

  4. No surprises

    You'll know the plan and the cost before we start. Nothing happens you didn't agree to.

Common questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most PPO dental plans. Call (801) 467-6555 with your plan name and we'll confirm whether we're in network before your visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

Ask us about our in-office membership plan. It's built for patients without dental insurance, and we'll walk you through it before you sign up.

What should I bring?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take.

It's been years since my last visit. Is that okay?

Completely. No lectures and no judgment. We'll start where you are. How we help nervous patients.
