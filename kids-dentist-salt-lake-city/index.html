A good start matters

How a child feels about the dentist often sticks with them for life. We're general dentists who see kids, and our goal for kids' dentistry in Salt Lake City is simple: a calm, stress-free visit your child feels good about.

We talk to kids in words they understand, show them what we're doing and go at their pace.

What kids' visits include

  • A gentle cleaning and a look at every tooth
  • Digital X-rays when they're needed
  • Sealants to protect deep grooves in back teeth
  • Tooth-colored fillings if we find a cavity
  • Simple tips for brushing, flossing and snacks

Tips for parents

Keep it positive at home. Skip words like "shot" or "hurt" and let us explain things in our own kid-friendly way. If your child is nervous, tell us ahead of time and we'll slow everything down.

Does your child breathe through their mouth, suck their thumb or have a tongue thrust? Ask us about myofunctional therapy.

Common questions

When should my child first see a dentist?

Around their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth coming in. If your child is older and hasn't been yet, it's never too late to start.

Are you a pediatric dentist?

No. We're general dentists who see kids, as part of a family practice that cares for all ages. That means parents and kids can come to the same office.

What if my child is scared?

That's normal. We go slow, explain things in kid-friendly terms and take breaks as needed. Let us know ahead of time and we'll plan for it.

Can I stay with my child during the visit?

Please call us at (801) 467-6555 to talk through what works for your child. We want both of you to feel comfortable.
