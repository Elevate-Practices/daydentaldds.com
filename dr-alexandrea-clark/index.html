Associate Dentist

Dr. Alexandrea Clark, DDS

Army veteranUSNS Comfort humanitarian missionU of U dental facultyAdmissions CommitteeFormer hygienist

Born and raised in Boise, Idaho, Dr. Clark has been in the dental field since 2010. She started as a dental assistant, earned her dental hygiene degree from Idaho State University, then went on to dental school at the University of Utah.

After graduating in 2018, she served as an Army dentist at Fort Bragg in North Carolina and deployed on a humanitarian mission aboard the USNS Comfort, providing care throughout Central and South America.

Today Dr. Clark splits her time between patient care at Day Dental and teaching in the University of Utah School of Dentistry's student clinic, where she also serves on the Admissions Committee screening and interviewing future dentists. She makes sure every patient feels comfortable and understands every step of their care.

Outside the office she's lifting weights, snowboarding, or walking her dog Karl with her husband Andrew.
