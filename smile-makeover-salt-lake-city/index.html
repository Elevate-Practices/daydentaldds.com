Built around you

A smile makeover in Salt Lake City blends several treatments into one custom plan, shaped around your goals and your natural features. It starts with a no-pressure conversation about what you'd like to change.

What a makeover can include

  • Teeth whitening
  • Porcelain veneers for chips, gaps and shape
  • Crowns to restore worn or broken teeth
  • Tooth-colored fillings to replace dark old ones
  • Gum care so everything starts healthy

Clear steps, clear costs

We lay out your plan step by step, with the cost of each part, so there are no surprises. You can do it all at once or in stages that fit your schedule and budget.

Common questions

How long does a smile makeover take?

Anywhere from a single visit to several months, depending on what's in your plan. We'll give you a timeline up front.

Can I do it in stages?

Yes. Many people spread treatment out to fit their schedule and budget.

Does insurance cover a smile makeover?

Cosmetic treatments usually aren't covered, but some parts, like crowns that repair damage, may be. We'll check your plan and give you the full cost first.
