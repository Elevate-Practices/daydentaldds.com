What myofunctional therapy is

Orofacial myofunctional therapy in Salt Lake City helps retrain the muscles of the tongue, lips and face. The goal is healthy oral posture and breathing habits, like resting with lips closed, breathing through the nose and swallowing correctly.

It's a little like physical therapy for your mouth, built on guided exercises you practice at home.

What it can help with

  • Mouth breathing
  • Open-mouth resting posture
  • Tongue thrust
  • Thumb sucking
  • Nail biting
  • Lip sucking

Who you'll work with

Brooke Werhli, RDH, is a dental hygienist and Orofacial Myofunctional Therapist on our team. She added myofunctional therapy to her skills so she could help patients build healthy muscle posture and airway habits.

Myo therapy can be part of care for kids and adults. For adults with snoring or sleep apnea, it may pair with our dental sleep medicine care.

Common questions

Is myofunctional therapy for kids or adults?

Both. Habits like mouth breathing, tongue thrust and thumb sucking can show up at any age.

What does therapy involve?

Simple exercises for the tongue, lips and face that you learn with Brooke and practice at home. We'll explain the plan for you before you start.

How do I know if my child needs it?

Watch for mouth breathing, an open-mouth resting posture, thumb sucking or a tongue that pushes forward when swallowing. Call (801) 467-6555 and we'll help you decide whether an evaluation makes sense.
