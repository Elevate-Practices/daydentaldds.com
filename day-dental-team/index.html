The rest of the crew

Everyone who makes your visit great.

Jessica MoraRegistered Dental Hygienist

Born and raised in Syracuse, Utah, Jessica has been in dentistry since 2009. She started as a dental assistant before earning her BS in Dental Hygiene from Weber State University in 2019, and completed a humanitarian mission in Ghana during her studies. She loves running, camping, traveling and a good book, and lives with her husband Damian and their daughter Olivia.

Brooke WerhliRDH · Orofacial Myofunctional Therapist

With 13 years of experience, Brooke is one of our most seasoned hygienists, and she's never stopped learning. She added orofacial myofunctional therapy to her skills to help patients build healthy oral muscle posture and airway habits. Outside work she's hiking, reading, or with her husband, two daughters and their growing family.

CamilleOffice Manager

With over five years in the dental field, Camille keeps everything running smoother than freshly polished enamel. She loves getting to know every patient and making sure they feel at home from the moment they walk in. Outside the office she's chasing her energetic twins and their dog Bruno, or hunting for treasure at the thrift store.

SavannahLead Dental Assistant

Savannah is the backbone of our back office. She makes sure every patient has a comfortable, positive visit while keeping the team running with care. When she's not at work, she's with her family and fiancé, boating, snowboarding, or enjoying a cozy night in.

DaliaDental Assistant

With over four years in the dental field, Dalia has usually triple-checked the schedule before you even ask. She's organized, detail-driven, and believes every great smile starts with compassion, with a touch of humor along the way.

Sophia FrancisDental Assistant

Born and raised in Southern California, Sophia has been in the dental field for about five years. She earned her Biology degree from the University of Utah and is working toward becoming a dentist. Outside the office she volunteers in her community, gets outdoors, and spends time with family, friends and her dog.
