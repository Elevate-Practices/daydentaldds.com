Dentures & partials in Salt Lake City

Natural-looking full and partial dentures, fitted with care so you can eat and smile with confidence.

Book online (801) 467-6555

Full or partial?

Full dentures replace all the teeth on the top or bottom. Partial dentures fill in a few missing teeth and fit around the teeth you still have. We design dentures in Salt Lake City for comfort, fit and a natural look, and help you pick what fits your mouth, budget and life.

Why replacing teeth matters

  • Chew a wider range of foods
  • Speak more clearly
  • Support your lips and cheeks
  • Smile without covering your mouth
  • Help keep remaining teeth from shifting

How it works

We take careful measurements, check the fit and look with you, and make adjustments until they feel right. New dentures take some getting used to, and we'll be with you through it.

Need a tooth removed first? We'll plan your extraction and your dentures together so you know the whole path and cost up front.

What to expect

Common questions

How long do dentures last?

Usually several years. Your mouth changes over time, so dentures may need adjusting or replacing. We check the fit at your visits.

My current dentures feel loose. Can you help?

Come see us. We'll check the fit, explain what's causing it and talk through your options.

Does insurance cover dentures?

Many PPO plans cover part of the cost. We'll go over your coverage first. No insurance? Ask about our membership plan.
