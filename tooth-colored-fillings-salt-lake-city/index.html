When you need a filling

A cavity is a small hole in your tooth caused by decay. Left alone, it grows and can reach the nerve. Tooth-colored fillings in Salt Lake City repair the cavity and bring back the tooth's strength, while blending in with your smile.

Signs to watch for

  • Sensitivity to sweets, hot or cold
  • A dark spot or hole you can see or feel
  • Food that keeps getting stuck in one spot
  • A twinge when you bite down

How it works

We numb the area, remove the decay and fill the space with a tooth-colored composite material shaped to fit your bite. You'll know what we're doing at every step, and you can ask us to pause any time.

Common questions

Do fillings hurt?

We numb the area first, so most people feel pressure but not pain. Tell us if you feel anything and we'll stop.

Can you replace my old metal fillings?

If an old filling is worn, cracked or leaking, we can replace it with a tooth-colored one. We'll tell you honestly whether it needs replacing.

Does insurance cover tooth-colored fillings?

Most PPO plans cover part of the cost. Coverage varies by plan, so we'll go over yours and the cost before we start.
