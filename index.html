Nervous about the dentist?

The Calm Visit Promise. Made for anxious patients.

Dr. Day did her residency at the Salt Lake City VA hospital, caring for veterans living with PTSD and dental anxiety. That's where our whole approach comes from.

  1. We explain first

    Before anything happens, we tell you what we see, what we'd suggest and why, in plain language.

  2. You set the pace

    Need a break? Raise your hand and we stop. No rushing, ever.

  3. No judgment

    Six months or six years since your last visit, we're just glad you're here. No lectures.

  4. No surprises

    You'll know the plan and the cost before we start. Nothing happens you didn't agree to.

Frequently asked questions

I get really nervous at the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Dr. Day did her residency at the VA hospital caring for patients with PTSD and dental anxiety, and our whole team is trained to slow down, explain everything in plain language and never rush you. Here's how we help nervous patients.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

We accept most PPO dental insurance plans. Call (801) 467-6555 and we'll confirm whether we're in network with your plan. No insurance? Ask about our in-office membership plan.

Do you see kids?

Yes. We're a family practice and see patients of all ages, from first checkups to grandparents. See kids' dentistry.

Can I get in the same day for an emergency?

We offer same-day appointments whenever possible for broken teeth, pain or swelling. Call (801) 467-6555 and tell us it's urgent. Emergency dental care.

What happens at my first visit?

A full exam, digital X-rays and a conversation about your concerns and goals. No pressure and no surprise treatment. What to expect as a new patient.

Where are you, and when are you open?

We're at 3001 S Richmond St, Ste A, Salt Lake City, UT 84106, near Sugar House and Millcreek. Open Monday and Friday 8 to 12, Tuesday through Thursday 8 to 5.
