Dentist & Practice Owner

Dr. Madison Day, DDS

VA hospital residencyPierre Fauchard Academy FellowIncisal Edge 40 Under 40Diplomate, ABDSMU of U dental faculty

Dr. Day grew up in West Jordan, Utah, earned her undergraduate degree at Southern Utah University, and graduated from the University of Utah School of Dentistry as part of its third graduating class. She then completed a general practice residency at the George E. Wahlen VA Medical Center in Salt Lake City, where she became known for her empathy and her care of patients with PTSD and anxiety. That experience shapes how she approaches every patient today.

Education is close to her heart. During residency she joined the Continuing Education planning committee at the U of U and guest lectured on biochemistry, physiology and immunology to first-year dental students. She was inducted into the Pierre Fauchard Academy in 2020, and in 2022 Incisal Edge named her one of its 40 Under 40 dentists in the nation. She was a guest lecturer on the Clinician's Report Accelerated Dentistry Update in November 2024 and November 2025, serves as Alumni President for the University of Utah School of Dentistry, and has spoken at its graduation and white coat ceremonies. Go Utes!

Most recently, Dr. Day became a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine, so she can help patients with snoring and sleep apnea using custom oral appliances. Learn about sleep apnea care.

At home, Dr. Day and her husband are kept busy by their two boys, Mack and Hank, two dogs, Bruce and Bones, and a cat named Lester. They love getting outside and soaking up the Utah landscape whenever they can.

Our promise to you

The Calm Visit Promise. For every patient, every visit.

However long it's been, here's what you can count on every time you're in our chair.

  1. We explain first

    Before anything happens, we tell you what we see, what we'd suggest and why, in plain language.

  2. You set the pace

    Need a break? Raise your hand and we stop. No rushing, ever.

  3. No judgment

    Six months or six years since your last visit, we're just glad you're here. No lectures.
