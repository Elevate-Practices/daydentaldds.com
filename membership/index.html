Common questions

Is the membership plan dental insurance?

No. It's an in-house savings plan run by Day Dental. You pay a monthly membership, and your preventive care is covered at our office with savings on other treatment.

How much does the membership cost?

Ask our front desk or call (801) 467-6555 for current membership pricing.

What does the membership cover?

Two doctor exams, two preventive cleanings, X-rays twice a year and fluoride twice a year are covered at 100%, plus a panoramic X-ray once every three years. You also save 50% on a third and fourth perio maintenance cleaning, 20% on fillings, crowns, root canals and extractions, 10% on dentures and 15% on cosmetic dentistry. Lab fee upgrades are not included.

Are there waiting periods or yearly maximums?

No. There are no yearly maximums, deductibles, claim forms, pre-authorizations or waiting periods.

How do I join?

Ask our front desk or call (801) 467-6555 to join.

Have insurance instead?

We accept most PPO plans too. Book your visit and we'll check your benefits.
