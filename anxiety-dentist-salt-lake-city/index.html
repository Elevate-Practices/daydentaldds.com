If the dentist makes your heart race, you're not alone, and you're not in trouble. We go slow, explain first and never judge.

You're in the right place

Lots of people put off the dentist because of fear, a bad past visit or embarrassment about how long it's been. If that's you, we get it. As an anxiety-friendly dentist in Salt Lake City, we built our whole practice around making visits feel calm, honest and in your control.

Whether it's been 6 months or 6 years, we're just glad you're here. No lectures. No guilt. Just a fresh start.

Care shaped by the VA

Dr. Madison Day did her general practice residency at the George E. Wahlen VA Medical Center here in Salt Lake City. There she cared for many patients living with PTSD and dental anxiety. She learned how to slow down, earn trust and make a dental chair feel safe again.

That experience shapes how our whole team works today. We don't treat anxiety as something to push through. We plan around it with you.

How we help you feel in control

  • We explain every step in plain language before we do it
  • You set the pace, and we never rush
  • Agree on a stop signal, like raising your hand, and we pause right away
  • Take breaks whenever you need them
  • Ask any question, as many times as you want
  • No pressure and no surprise treatment. You hear the plan and the cost first.

Want to know about comfort options? Ask us. We'll talk through what we can do for you before your visit, so you know what to expect.

What a first visit looks like

Your first visit can be as simple as a conversation. Tell us what worries you, what happened before and what would help. Then, when you're ready, we do an exam and digital X-rays and walk you through anything we find.

Nothing happens without your okay. If you want to start with just a gentle cleaning and checkup, that is a great first step. See what to expect on our new patient page.

Hurting right now?

Anxiety often means a small problem gets put off until it hurts. If you have pain or swelling, please don't wait. Call (801) 467-6555. We offer same-day visits for urgent dental problems whenever possible, and we'll be just as gentle when it's urgent.

Our promise to you

The Calm Visit Promise. Made for anxious patients.

Dr. Day did her residency at the Salt Lake City VA hospital, caring for veterans living with PTSD and dental anxiety. That's where our whole approach comes from.

  1. We explain first

    Before anything happens, we tell you what we see, what we'd suggest and why, in plain language.

  2. You set the pace

    Need a break? Raise your hand and we stop. No rushing, ever.

  3. No judgment

    Six months or six years since your last visit, we're just glad you're here. No lectures.

  4. No surprises

How we help anxious patients

Common questions

I get really nervous at the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Dr. Day trained at the Salt Lake VA caring for patients with PTSD and dental anxiety, and our whole team slows down, explains everything and never rushes you. You stay in control the entire visit.

Do you offer sedation or nitrous?

Please ask us about comfort options when you call. We'll talk through what we can do for you before your visit so there are no surprises.

It's been years since I've been to a dentist. Will I get a lecture?

No. Whether it's been 6 months or 6 years, we're glad you came in. We focus on where you are now and what's next, without judgment.

Can I stop if I feel overwhelmed?

Yes, any time. We agree on a stop signal before we start, and we pause right away when you use it. Breaks are always okay.

Can I just come in to talk first?

Yes. Call (801) 467-6555 and tell us you're nervous. We'll plan a first visit that starts with a conversation and moves only as fast as you're comfortable.
