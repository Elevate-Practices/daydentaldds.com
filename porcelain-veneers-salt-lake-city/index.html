What veneers can fix

Porcelain veneers in Salt Lake City are thin, custom shells bonded to the front of your teeth. They're ideal if you want a natural-looking smile upgrade.

  • Chips and small cracks
  • Stains that whitening can't lift
  • Small gaps between teeth
  • Teeth that are worn, short or uneven

It starts with an honest consult

We start by talking about what bugs you about your smile and what you'd love it to look like. Then we tell you honestly whether veneers are the right fit, or whether something simpler, like whitening, would get you there.

If veneers make sense, we prepare the teeth, take careful measurements and have your veneers made to match your face, bite and shade. You'll know the full plan and cost before we begin.

Caring for veneers

Brush, floss and keep up with regular cleanings. Avoid using your teeth as tools. Nervous about cosmetic work? We offer gentle care for dental anxiety for every kind of visit.

Common questions

Do veneers look fake?

Not when they're planned well. We match shape and shade to your face and your other teeth for a natural look.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

With good care, often many years. We'll give you a realistic idea for your case.

Does insurance cover veneers?

Usually not, because veneers are considered cosmetic. We'll give you the full cost up front so you can plan.
