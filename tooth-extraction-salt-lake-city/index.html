When a tooth needs to come out

We always try to save a natural tooth first. When that isn't possible, a tooth extraction in Salt Lake City can stop pain and infection and protect the teeth around it.

  • A tooth too broken or decayed to repair
  • A tooth loosened by advanced gum disease
  • An infection that can't be treated another way
  • Crowding that affects nearby teeth

What to expect

We numb the area well, so you feel pressure but not pain. We explain each step before we do it and stop whenever you need a break.

You'll go home with clear aftercare instructions, and we're a phone call away if anything feels off while you heal.

What comes next

Losing a tooth can let nearby teeth shift over time. Right away, we'll walk you through your options for replacing it, such as a partial or full denture, and what each costs.

If your case would be better handled by another provider, we'll tell you why and help you with the next step.

Common questions

Does getting a tooth pulled hurt?

The area is numb during the extraction, so you should feel pressure, not pain. Some soreness afterward is normal for a few days.

How long does it take to heal?

Most people feel back to normal in a few days. The gum and bone keep healing for a few weeks.

Do you remove wisdom teeth?

We'll check your wisdom teeth at your exam and tell you whether they should come out. Then we'll talk through the right way to handle it for your situation.

Should I replace a pulled tooth?

Often, yes, especially if you chew or smile with it. We'll explain your options so you can decide.
